Section 2

Issues Identified — Corrections Required Before External Use

Eight issues were found. None undermine the strategic recommendations. All should be corrected before the document is shared with external partners, investors, or advisors.

1 — Poll & Video Format Multipliers Are Off

The document states Polls = 1.19x and Video = 1.05x. AuthoredUp's actual data shows Polls ≈ 1.64x (with a 35% YoY decline noted) and Video ≈ 1.10x for company pages.

Fix: Remove the specific multipliers for Polls and Video. Describe the trend in prose instead: "polls and video underperform documents and images on company pages."

2 — "Most Significant Algorithm Shift" Quote Is Unsourced

Attributed to Sprout Social. Sprout Social's article does not contain this language. The underlying point is accurate and well-supported by other data in the document.

Fix: Remove the phrase or reframe as a synthesis claim without attributing it to Sprout Social.

3 — 6x Conversion Lift Misattributed to Sopro

The statistic is accurate but originates from LinkedIn's own marketing research, not Sopro. Sopro cited it from LinkedIn.

Fix: Change attribution to "LinkedIn Marketing Solutions."

4 — 61% Conversion Lift Also Misattributed to Sopro

Same issue as above. "Followers exposed to both organic and paid content are 61% more likely to convert" is LinkedIn's own data, cited through Sopro.

Fix: Change attribution to "LinkedIn Marketing Solutions."

5 — Newsletter Open Rate Upper Bound (60%) Unconfirmed

The 40% figure is confirmed. The 60% upper bound is not substantiated. Additionally, LinkedIn measures "clicks to read" — not the same metric as email open rates — making direct comparison misleading.

Fix: Cite "approximately 40% open rate" and add a note that LinkedIn and email metrics are not directly comparable.

6 — PostNitro's 11.2x Impressions Claim Is a Metric Conflation

The 11.2x figure is derived from engagement rate differentials, not a direct impression comparison. LinkedIn data suggests carousels generate 1.5–3x more impressions than text posts in direct comparisons.

Fix: Clarify that 11.2x refers to engagement lift, not raw impressions. Update the framing accordingly.

7 — LinkedIn Live "30% More Likely to Interact" Misattributed to Expandi

This statistic is from LinkedIn's own 2024 data, not Expandi's H1 2025 outreach report. The Expandi attribution is incorrect.

Fix: Change attribution to "LinkedIn Business Blog (2024)."

8 — 150K Follower Newsletter Case Study Cannot Be Verified ⚠

The claim that a 150K-follower page generated 40K newsletter subscribers in two weeks (27% conversion), attributed to Social Media Examiner, could not be located. Social Media Examiner's own newsletter achieved a 14.6% conversion rate — significantly lower. This is the only claim in the document that may be fabricated.

Action required: Remove this case study entirely before external sharing. If a newsletter conversion benchmark is needed, replace with Social Media Examiner's documented 14.6% rate or the verified 15.6% from the 6,486-follower case study.

Bottom Line for the Team

This research document is a strong, well-sourced foundation. The six strategic priorities in Section 11 of the original document (newsletter launch, dual-exposure paid stack, 5-pillar content calendar, warm-path outreach, event content funnels, and page role reframe) are all grounded in verified data and remain fully actionable as written.

Apply the corrections above — eight targeted edits — and the document is ready for external use, board presentation, or member communication.
